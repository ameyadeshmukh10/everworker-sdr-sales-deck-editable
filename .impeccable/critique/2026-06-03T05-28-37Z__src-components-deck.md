## Anti-Patterns Verdict
Mostly does NOT look AI-generated: 7 genuinely different compositions across 12 slides, avoiding the identical-card-grid trap. One concentrated tell: all 12 headlines use the same machine (uppercase kicker + headline whose last phrase is hero-gradient-text). Gradient-clipped text is on the absolute-ban list; brand-sanctioned but overused on every slide, which flattens emphasis and is the clearest templated signal.
Deterministic scan: unavailable (bundled detector not found after real attempt). No overlay (ships with missing bundle). Fallback: manual review of 12 screenshots + source.

## Overall Impression
Strong, on-brand deck that does its core job (glanceable, diagram-led visual support). High craft, real variety. Biggest opportunity: editorial restraint at the deck level — every slide shouts equally (same kicker, gradient headline, light bg), so no rhythm, no peak, no quiet.

## What's Working
1. Composition variety — 7 different structures across 12 slides.
2. Credible product UI — Primary-inbox card (3), LinkedIn activity card (5), real provider marks.
3. Purposeful motion — flowing connectors, breathing core, marching comet, curve draw-on.

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Gradient-headline formula on all 12 slides. Reserve hero-gradient-text for ~3 peaks (opener, case study, closing); solid ink + weight/size elsewhere. Command: typeset.
- [P1] Slides 9 & 12 are reading slides, not glance slides. Split each into two, or cut secondary numbers (one hero stat + 3 points on 9; tiers-only on 12, move included/upgrade to talk track/follow-up). Command: distill.
- [P2] No cover slide and no closing/CTA slide; deck ends on a pricing table (flat per peak-end). Add title slide + closing next-steps slide. Command: craft.
- [P2] Tonally flat across all 12 (all warm-light). Art-direct 1-2 dark "peak" slides (case study and/or closing). Command: colorize / bolder.
- [P3] 12 unlabeled progress dots; no fast jump to a section on demand. Group dots into labeled sections or add jump key. Command: layout.

## Minor Observations

- Low-contrast small sublabels (~0.45-0.5 ink opacity) struggle in bright rooms / Zoom.
- Slide 8 slightly top-weighted vs others; vertical rhythm not uniform.
