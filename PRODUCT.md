# PRODUCT

register: brand

## Product purpose
Everworker sells AI Workers that drive revenue — autonomous agents that run real
work inside a customer's existing stack (CRM, signals, sequencing). The flagship is
the **SDR AI Worker**: an always-on AI sales-development rep that turns signals and
inbound leads into booked meetings, generating 3–5x more meetings per rep without
adding headcount. Other workers cover marketing and finance.

## Users
- **Primary (this deck): salespeople presenting live on a call.** Slides are visual
  aids behind a human narrating. They must be glanceable, light on text, and carry the
  point with a diagram or a believable piece of product UI, not paragraphs.
- **Audience of the deck: CROs, VP Sales, RevOps leaders** evaluating whether to trust
  an autonomous worker with their pipeline. Skeptical, time-poor, outcome-driven.

## Tone / voice
Confident, calm, premium. "On-brand, on-message, never sleeps." Show the machine
working; let outcomes (meetings booked, pipeline doubled) speak. Never hypey or
cluttered. Three brand-voice words: **precise, alive, effortless.**

## Strategic principles
- One idea per slide. The salesperson is the narrator; the slide is the proof.
- Show product/diagram over prose. Motion implies the worker is *running*, not static.
- Outcome-anchored: every capability ladders up to "more meetings, more pipeline."

## Anti-references
- Not a dense corporate slideware deck (no bullet walls, no clip-art icons-in-circles).
- Not generic dark "AI" aesthetic (neon-on-black, circuit motifs). Everworker is light,
  warm, and human.
- Not a cluttered dashboard screenshot dump.

## Identity lock (do not "improve" away)
The brand has shipped a committed identity. Preserve it; this is not greenfield:
- **Gilroy** typeface, emerald **#22826F** + mint **#52FEBF** accent, warm near-white
  backgrounds, near-black ink **#0F1C18**, glassmorphism, soft long shadows.
- The existing `hero-gradient-text` (emerald→mint) is an established brand element and
  is allowed despite the generic gradient-text ban — identity-preservation wins. Use it
  only where the live site already does (key headline words), never as body grammar.
